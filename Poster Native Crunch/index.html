1. Product Features

Crispy Colombian tropical fruit chips, available in three flavors: pineapple, mango, and banana. A convenient snack to enjoy at any time, with no frying.

🍍 Flavors: Pineapple, mango, and banana.
✨ Texture: Crispy, like a traditional chip.
🌱 Main ingredients: Dehydrated fruit.
📦 Presentation: Individual 30 g bag.
💚 Benefits: Natural fruit, frying-free process.
🕒 Occasions: Between meals, at the office, at university, before or after training.

2. Target Market

Primary Market: Young adults and adults aged 18 to 35 in Cartagena and Turbaco.

Segment Profile What they look for
University students 18 to 25 years old Affordable, convenient snacks that look good on social media
Young professionals 25 to 35 years old Healthy options for the office, willing to pay a bit more for quality
Active people 18 to 35 years old Natural snacks for energy, at the gym or while exercising

Secondary Market: Parents looking for snacks with no added sugar for their children, and vegetarian or vegan consumers.

Psychographic Profile: People who care about their diet without giving up flavor, who value local products and prefer brands with their own identity.

3. Positioning Strategies

🌿 Brand Pillars

  • Origin: Colombian tropical fruit, with a Caribbean identity.
  • Naturalness: Fruit as the main ingredient, no frying.
  • Convenience: A snack for any place and moment.
  • Flavor: Crunchy, with recognizable flavors.

🎯 Differentiation & Price

  • Vs. Economy nut mixes: Made from fruit, with a crispy texture.
  • Vs. Premium brands (Good Chips): More accessible price and local origin.
  • Base Price: $6,900 COP per 30 g bag.

🚀 Sales Channels by Phase

  • Phase 1: Social media, WhatsApp, cafés, health food stores, allied gyms & universities in Cartagena.
  • Phase 2: Delivery apps & neighborhood stores in Cartagena and Turbaco.
  • Phase 3: Supermarkets (when volume & costs are defined).

💪 Gym Partnerships & Promotion

  • Gyms: Reception POS, post-workout samples, 10% member discount code, trainer ambassadors & sports sponsorships.
  • Promotion: Free tastings, Instagram/TikTok short videos, and Cartagena fitness/healthy microinfluencers.
  • Key Messages: "Crispy, healthy, and delicious" · "Made with Colombian fruit" · "Your snack to go".
